# /dashboard vs `dashboard`

**Template:** `design-system/templates/dashboard/Dashboard.dc.html` — teal `AppHeader`, title block, a "Needs Attention" card rail (urgent red-bar / warning gold-bar cards), a 300px Quick Start column of four card-buttons, and the design-system `ActivityFeed` with "Load more". Its `@template description` promises "time-filtered KPI cards with trend deltas"; **the markup has no KPI cards, no deltas and no period control** — treat that sentence as stale intent.
**App:** `/dashboard` → `src/app/dashboard/page.tsx` (client; `useSession`, `fetch('/api/dashboard')`, `selectedPeriod` state) and `src/components/ActivityFeed.tsx` (client; `useActivityFeed()` → `GET /api/activity-feed?limit=500`, 24-case `getActivityTitle()` switch, `getActivityColor()` hue map, numbered pagination). Chrome from `src/app/layout.tsx` (`Navigation.tsx` + inline footer).
**Data sources:** `GET /api/dashboard` → `userSites[] {id,name,address,isSingleSeniorOnly}`, `monthlyMetrics` and `allTimeMetrics` `{events, participants, distributions, adminHours}` (tables `users`, `sites`, `events`, `supply_distributions`); `GET /api/activity-feed` → rows of `{id, type, user{firstName,lastName}, timestamp (pre-formatted "3 days ago"), details (metadata jsonb), targetId}` from `activity_feed` ⋈ `users`. `activity-feed.service.ts` writes 25 `activity_type` values.

Behaviour to keep: the `status === 'unauthenticated'` redirect, the `/api/dashboard` fetch with its loading and "Try Again" error states, the This Month / All Time switch and the four metrics it drives, the admin-only navigation card (the only in-app path to `/admin/*` while `Navigation.tsx`'s admin items are commented out), the 500-row feed fetch, and every sentence the feed can produce.
